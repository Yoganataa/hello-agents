# Ideas parking lot

Every "it would be nice if…" goes here instead of into the code. Agents append;
only the human promotes an idea, and only at a version boundary.

## Rules

1. New idea mid-task: add a line below, then go back to the task. Don't build it.
2. Promoting an idea to scope needs a trade-off in `decisions.md`: what gets cut, how much time it adds, or what it costs.
3. Ideas with no evidence stay parked. Evidence means users, data, or a repeated real need.
